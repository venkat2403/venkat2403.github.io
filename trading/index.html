Fair value is the VMI (Adam Khoo 7-step) valuation — a 20-year 3-stage projection at a 10% discount rate. A name whose sales, net income and cash flow are not all consistently rising gets no value at all rather than a number, because every method would extrapolate a trend that is not in the data. Buy at or below fair value; never pay more than +25% above it. 10X scores the chance of a ten-year 10X after share dilution (70+ strong, under 50 weak). Trend is step 7: a confirmed uptrend needs the 50MA above the 150MA, price above the 200MA, and the 200MA rising — all three. A name below fair value in a confirmed downtrend reads "cheap but falling" and is not a buy. Step 3 (economic moat) is qualitative — no data feed returns it. Judge it yourself: brand monopoly, switching costs, network effect, barriers to entry, economies of scale.
