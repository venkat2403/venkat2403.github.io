Pre-commercial scenario valuation

For companies with no positive free cash flow, operating cash flow or net income, where a 20-year discounted model has nothing to discount. This is an explicit scenario you control — not a DCF, and not OracleValue.

Pre-Commercial Scenario IV
—
Enter assumptions and calculate.
Future Revenue = Revenue Anchor × (1 + Revenue CAGR)^Years Future Equity Value = Future Revenue × Exit P/S + Cash − Debt Present Equity Value = Future Equity Value ÷ (1 + Discount Rate)^Years Scenario IV / Share = Present Equity Value ÷ Diluted Shares
Revenue anchor defaults to max(TTM revenue, latest quarterly revenue × 4). Every field is an assumption: the exit multiple and CAGR move the answer more than anything else here. Read the output as one scenario, not as a valuation.
